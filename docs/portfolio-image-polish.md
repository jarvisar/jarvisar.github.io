# Portfolio cover polish

Ten older project covers were edited with the built-in imagegen tool. The brief was conservative: retain each original identity and color palette while simplifying typography, removing heavy text effects, and improving spacing. These are AI-assisted edits, not pixel-identical reproductions of the original images. Original assets and project screenshots remain available unchanged.

Final web assets are JPEGs at 1200 × 675, except Mini Cities at 1200 × 1200 to preserve its square collage. The portfolio and existing detail-page title cards use the new assets. Coastline, CityDriver, 3D Map Creator, laserSVG and involvement images were left as they were.
