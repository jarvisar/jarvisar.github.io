About

An endless driving game built with Three.js. Roads and scenery generate as you drive through seven routes, from coastal highways and a rainy downtown to snowy mountains and volcanic landscapes. Each route has its own car, weather, and landmarks to find along the way. All the terrain, models, and audio are generated in code, with no downloaded assets. Includes a garage, multiple camera views, autodrive, and support for keyboard, touch, controllers, and browser VR. Also available as a desktop app.
